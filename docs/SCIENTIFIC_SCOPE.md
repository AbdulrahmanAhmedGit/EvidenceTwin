# Scientific scope and authority

The [approved concepts brief](../concepts_brief.md) is the requirements baseline. [PRODUCT.md](../PRODUCT.md) is product context; [design_draft.md](../design_draft.md) and the [scientific design family](designs/backend-completion/) retain selected directions, proposals and approval history. All **24 gates remain OPEN**: D01–D09, E01–E07 and P01–P08 in the [gate register](designs/backend-completion/TECHNICAL_PLAN_GATES.md). Local application improvements do not close them.

Current production behavior is exploratory ingestion and model review: preserved evidence bytes/hashes/revisions, bounded CSV/JSON parsing, original-image inspection, separately prompted reviewers, cross-review, model adjudication, audit and exploratory reports. Parsing, schema/reference checks and execution completion are application checks. The current descriptive-evidence/2 extension retains complete-table queries, scoped Decimal comparisons and permitted source retrieval. These do not establish source authenticity, methodological comparability or validated statistical inference. See [evidence processing](EVIDENCE_PROCESSING.md). Reports retain `not_evaluated` scientific assessment. Model arithmetic and agreement are AI interpretation.

Source authentication, validated scientific/statistical procedures, Blind Optics/Neutral Twin generation, benchmark scoring and a product MCP service remain unavailable. Newly authorized runs can retrieve supported public URLs under bounded network/access checks. Retrieval is separate from authenticity and content verification. A PDF is an attachment without extraction. XLSX is planned by the baseline but not accepted by this runtime. Image-only review cannot establish exact numerical/statistical verification. Missing evidence is not contradictory evidence.

The target distinguishes source provenance, applicability/performed-check status, verification coverage, AI interpretation, claim assessment and historical/current outcomes. Keep these separate in UI and reports. Never turn the local case model, auth boundary or PostgreSQL infrastructure tests into evidence that the target Scientific Core exists or that a gate is closed.

The nested design family includes numerical/decimal/statistical/source specifications, typed check axes/absence, claim confirmation/revision, Council and acceptance-corpus contracts, publication/transaction/lifecycle designs, infrastructure schema/tooling/test plans and raw Goldfish reviews. Their statuses and append-only decisions remain intact. PostgreSQL, SQLAlchemy Core/Psycopg/Alembic and Read Committed are selected bounded infrastructure directions; earlier candidate-only paragraphs are chronology. Exact scientific schemas/procedure acceptance and hosted operating policy remain unresolved. Consult later scoped decision records, not isolated early wording, without broadening that scope.

The selected [Ember acceptance investigation](designs/backend-completion/ember-2025-acceptance/acceptance_case.md) preserves manual reference research and artifact provenance. Publication-time dataset/methodology identity and accessible original-chart evidence remain incomplete. It has not passed an EvidenceTwin scientific execution. Historical support and latest-data disagreement must remain separate.

Development examples/fixtures and temporary synthetic-provider tests establish only their stated application behavior. No paid-provider, independent scientific, hosted, physical-device, screen-reader or cross-browser validation is claimed. The owner performs final acceptance; scientific/pilot enablement requires its own approved specifications and evidence.

Public caption access through youtube-transcript-api and HTML extraction through Scrapling are source-processing operations. Captions preserve their original language and generated/manual provenance. Neither access nor parsing authenticates scientific claims. LibreTranslate report derivatives are explicitly machine translated. Numeric/reference protection can retain unsafe-to-translate passages in their original wording, producing a labeled partial translation. Scientific terminology still requires human review. Historical report wording and findings are preserved.

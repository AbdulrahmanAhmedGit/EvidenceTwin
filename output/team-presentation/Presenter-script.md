# EvidenceTwin Council team presentation

Short English scripts for ten slides.

## Slide 1 — EvidenceTwin Council

Today I will show the working product, explain our focus on misleading scientific visualizations, and outline the scientific work still needed before independent reconstruction can become a trusted capability.

## Slide 2 — Core product focus

Our main question is whether a chart could mislead its reader. We begin with the original visualization, then use data and scientific context to examine the specific concern.

## Slide 3 — New investigation

The new composer puts chart upload first. Researchers can add data, links and context through compact controls. Saving creates a real investigation, while AI review requires a separate consent step.

## Slide 4 — Visual results

Results now prioritize the original plot and a specific recorded visual concern. These are AI observations that require supporting evidence. A visual concern does not automatically establish that a chart is misleading.

## Slide 5 — Working evidence and reports

The application preserves evidence and connects it to review records. It can inspect supported tables, retrieve accessible sources and produce reports. Translation is clearly labeled and retains original passages when safe translation cannot be confirmed.

## Slide 6 — Live demonstration

For the live demo, upload the educational bar chart and its actual dataset. Examine the ten-percent baseline, check the recorded values, and confirm that the app explains its reconstruction limitations honestly.

## Slide 7 — Engineering status

We have checked builds, focused application behavior and rendered pages. The image-only workflow saved real evidence in isolated storage. These checks establish engineering behavior. They do not establish scientific accuracy or readiness for a hosted pilot.

## Slide 8 — Current scientific limits

We must keep these boundaries explicit. A flagged visual feature is a concern to examine. Reconstruction needs an approved method, and source access alone cannot establish authenticity. We will avoid presenting missing evidence as a negative finding.

## Slide 9 — Planned reconstruction workflow

This is the intended workflow. The next major scientific component is Blind Optics. It should reconstruct from original numerical data under a documented blind protocol, then compare the result with the original chart.

## Slide 10 — Next team decisions

The team can now review the working interface and agree on the scientific scope. Our next decisions concern the blind protocol, a defensible healthcare evaluation set, supported reconstruction templates and the conditions for a controlled pilot.
